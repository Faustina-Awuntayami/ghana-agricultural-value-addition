# Agricultural Value Addition and Commercialization among Ghanaian Farm Households

This repository contains an independent empirical research project examining the relationship between household-level agricultural processing and agricultural commercialization in Ghana using nationally representative data from the Ghana Living Standards Survey Round 7 (GLSS 7).

## Research Question

> What is the relationship between household processing of agricultural produce and agricultural commercialization among farm households in Ghana?

## Objectives

* Identify the characteristics of farm households engaged in agricultural processing.
* Examine the agricultural products processed by Ghanaian households.
* Investigate whether processing participation is associated with agricultural sales and commercialization.
* Explore regional and gender differences in processing activities.

## Data

* **Primary dataset:** Ghana Living Standards Survey Round 7 (GLSS 7), Ghana Statistical Service.
* **Secondary data:** Ministry of Food and Agriculture (MOFA) agricultural statistics.

**Note:** Raw GLSS data are **not included** in this repository because they are subject to the Ghana Statistical Service's data access and redistribution terms.

## Methods

This project will use:

* Descriptive statistics
* Binary choice models (Logit/Probit)
* Ordinary Least Squares (OLS)
* Heterogeneity analysis
* Survey-weighted estimation where appropriate
